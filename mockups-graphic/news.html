Releases and project news

News

What has changed in the model, the documentation and the projects around them. This is the GreenREFORM-EU feed, not DREAM's Danish news.

  • October 2026

    Version 2.0 published as open source

    The model code, the theoretical documentation and the user guide are now available from the repository. The release closes the KR Foundation project.

  • September 2026

    New Technical Support Instrument project announced

    The Commission has announced the successor project, which will develop country-specific sub-models with a wider group of Member States. Scope and participants follow the official announcement.

  • June 2026

    The energy technology model gets a new data module

    Technology catalogues can now be maintained outside the GAMS source, which makes it easier for a country team to bring in its own cost and capacity data.

  • 25 February 2026

    Final paper from the TSI project

    The paper presents the model together with two policy experiments: a carbon tax with revenue recycling, run with and without the technology block. It stays in Analyses after the news item ages.
