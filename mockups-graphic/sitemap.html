Proposed structure

GreenREFORM-EU — sitemap

Seven pages in the primary navigation. Applications is a hub holding three kinds of object: analyses, projects and country teams.

Analyses is where a researcher or official starts: has anyone scored a question like mine? Projects and country teams are the map around it.

News announces a paper. The analysis record keeps it. Newsletter signup repeats in the footer of every page.

No page per documentation chapter. The document itself is the artefact, offered as PDF, HTML and source.
