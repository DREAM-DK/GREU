# Raw EU-wide statistics on NACE E37-E39 output composition — Sweden 2020

> **Not committed to Git.** The raw JSON-stat responses are excluded by the
> `data/preprocessing/data/*_raw/` rule in `.gitignore`; only this README,
> `manifest.json` and `eu27_coverage_probe.json` are versioned. Re-create the
> directory with
> `python data/preprocessing/scripts/download_waste_stats_se_2020.py`.

Feasibility-probe inputs for characterizing the Sweden 2020 public-core
`CPA_E37-E39` monetary residual (supply-side control 60.156 bn SEK, use-side
residual 54.357 bn SEK): how much of the waste/sewerage industry's output is
plausibly energy-relevant, using only public EU-wide sources?

Downloaded 2026-07-31 by
`data/preprocessing/scripts/download_waste_stats_se_2020.py` from the Eurostat
dissemination API
(`https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/api/dissemination/statistics/1.0/data/<code>`).
Raw JSON-stat responses are preserved byte-for-byte; exact URLs, query
filters, SHA-256 hashes and observation counts are in `manifest.json`.

## Files

| File | Dataset | Content |
|---|---|---|
| `sbs_na_ind_r2_SE_2020.json` | SBS annual detailed enterprise statistics (2008–2020 series) | Turnover, production value, value added, employment etc. for NACE E36–E39 down to 4-digit detail (E381/E382/E383 …), million EUR |
| `env_wasgen_SE_2020.json` | Waste generation | Tonnes generated by NACE activity, total and excl. major mineral wastes |
| `env_wastrt_SE_2020.json` | Waste treatment | Tonnes treated by operation: landfill, incineration (D10), **energy recovery (R1)**, recycling, backfilling |
| `env_ac_pepsgg1_SE_2020.json` | EPEA production, general government + NPISH | Output of environmental protection services by CEPA domain (CEPA2 wastewater, CEPA3 waste), MIO_EUR + MIO_NAC |
| `env_ac_pepssp1_SE_2020.json` | EPEA production, specialist producers | Market output of EP services by CEPA domain, MIO_EUR + MIO_NAC |
| `env_ac_egss2_SE_2020.json` | Environmental goods and services sector | Output/value added/exports by NACE section × CEPA/CReMA, incl. **CReMA 13A production of energy from renewable sources** |
| `nrg_bal_c_SE_2020.json` | Complete energy balance (waste fuels only) | TJ of W6100/W6210/W6220 waste fuels: production, transformation input to electricity/heat by plant type, final consumption |
| `eu27_coverage_probe.json` | — | One-cell 2020 availability probe of the six datasets above for all EU-27 members |
| `manifest.json` | — | Provenance: URLs, filters, retrieval date, SHA-256, negative probes |

## Negative probes (recorded in `manifest.json`)

- `sbs_ovw_act`, `sbs_sc_ovw`: the post-2021 SBS series exist but hold **no
  2020 observations** — the 2008–2020 series `sbs_na_ind_r2` must be used.
- `env_ac_epneec`, `env_ac_pepsnsp1`: HTTP 404, not dissemination datasets.
  EPEA production is published as `env_ac_pepsgg1` / `env_ac_pepssp1` only;
  the ancillary-output-of-non-specialists table is not disseminated.

## Notable observations

- Sweden does not report the EPEA item `EPS_REC_BYPR` ("related
  non-environmental-protection output", i.e. by-product revenue such as sold
  energy) — the one EPEA item that would directly quantify energy-relevant
  revenue is absent for SE 2020.
- SBS 4-digit detail below E382 (E3821/E3822, where incineration sits) is not
  published for SE 2020; E382 is only available as a 3-digit aggregate.
- `nrg_bal_c` shows the waste fuel burned for energy in Sweden 2020 is burned
  almost entirely in **main-activity producer** CHP/heat plants, which are
  classified in NACE D35, not E38.
- EU-27 coverage gaps found by the probe are cell-level confidentiality, not
  dataset-level: CY and IE publish SBS E37/E39 but suppress division E38; AT
  publishes `env_wastrt` 2020 but not the RCV_E cell for the non-mineral
  aggregate. Only Luxembourg is absent from EGSS altogether.
